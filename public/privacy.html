Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. Applies to the Protocol app and app.maan.life.

The short version

Protocol collects the information it needs to build and adapt your weekly training and nutrition plan, and nothing else. We do not run ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not share your health information with anyone for marketing. You can delete your account and everything in it from inside the app.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a password. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Profile: what you enter during onboarding: age, height, weight, sex, training objective, experience level, weekly activities and their schedule, available equipment, and any injuries you note.
  • Check-ins and logs: your weekly check-in (waist measurement, body weight, energy, sleep, joint notes, how training felt) and any workouts or daily tracking you choose to log.
  • Technical basics: standard server logs (such as IP address and device type) kept briefly by our hosting providers for security and reliability.

We do not collect your contacts, photos, location, or anything from other apps.

How we use it

One purpose: generating your plan. Your profile sets the template, and each check-in adjusts the next week (carbs, training volume, exercise swaps). Your email is used to sign you in and, rarely, to tell you something important about your account. That's it.

Your health and fitness information is never used for advertising and never shared with third parties for their marketing.

Where your data lives

Your data is stored with Supabase (our database and authentication provider) on servers in the United States, and the app is served by Vercel. Both act as processors on our behalf. Data is encrypted in transit, and database rules ensure each account can only ever read its own rows.

Affiliate links

Some exercise videos and supplement items in the app link to outside retailers, including Amazon. These open in your browser, and we may earn a commission on qualifying purchases. The retailers do not receive your Protocol data; what you buy there is between you and them. As an Amazon Associate, Protocol earns from qualifying purchases.

Your controls

  • Edit your profile and activities at any time in the app.
  • Delete your account in the More tab. This permanently removes your profile, check-ins, and logs from our database.
  • Or email us (address below) and we will delete it for you.

We keep your data for as long as your account exists, and we delete it when you do.

A few more things

Not medical advice. Protocol is a fitness and nutrition planning tool, not a medical service. Talk to your physician before starting a new training or supplement program.

Age. Protocol is built for adults and is not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

Changes. If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will note it in the app and update the date at the top of this page.
